# Recent Real-World Evidence: US Peptide / Research-Chemical Card Processing (Approvals & Terminations)

**Scope:** US merchant + US customers, CARD merchant accounts specifically. Business profile: research-use-only (RUO) peptides + lab supplies, ~$10k/mo, ~$60 AOV.
**Access date:** 2026-10-20. Source dates are stated separately below.
**Labeling key:** VERIFIED CURRENT / PLAUSIBLE — CONFIRMATION NEEDED / WEAK-EXCLUDE.

**Overall reliability caveat:** The open web for this vertical is dominated by high-risk-processor marketing, affiliate SEO, and AI-spun content farms (notably `stonevillenc.org`, which returned garbled/duplicated text and should be treated as WEAK-EXCLUDE). Reddit and Medium — the two sources most likely to hold genuine merchant anecdotes — were **not accessible** this session (see Gaps). So almost everything below is provider/vendor-sourced supporting evidence, not independent merchant testimony.

## Q1. Which processors/ISOs do peptide/RC sellers report being APPROVED by, and which do they report being DROPPED/FROZEN by?

### Takeaway
No independent (merchant-voice) approval data was reachable. Vendor/industry pages consistently name Stripe, PayPal, Square, Shopify Payments, and Adyen as processors that **refuse or close** peptide accounts, and point sellers toward high-risk specialists (AllayPay, QuadraPay named; Corepay, EasyPayDirect, PaylineData, PeptiPay, Binderr-as-matcher market themselves). Critically for THIS business: at least one named high-risk processor (Corepay) **explicitly excludes RUO / "for research purposes" peptides**.

### Cited Findings — Terminations / refusals (mainstream)
- "PayPal, Stripe, and Square now refuse to work with peptide merchants" and classify the industry as high-risk — advocacy newsletter, dated **Oct 22, 2025** — [Project Biohacking (beehiiv)](https://projectbiohacking.beehiiv.com/p/the-fda-s-peptide-crackdown-what-every-biohacker-needs-to-know). PLAUSIBLE — CONFIRMATION NEEDED (secondary, no merchant names).
- Binderr names Stripe, PayPal, Shopify Payments, and **Adyen** as platforms that restrict or decline peptide sellers — dated **published Sept 21, 2026, updated Oct 8, 2026** — [Binderr](https://binderr.com/marketplace/high-risk-payment-processors-for-peptides). PLAUSIBLE — CONFIRMATION NEEDED (vendor marketing).
- Stripe's public restricted-businesses list does **not** name "peptides" or "SARMs" by that word; it restricts "prescription-only" drugs/online pharmacies and prohibits "unsafe" nutraceuticals/supplements and improperly labeled "research chemicals" (page returned Thai-localized text; © 2026, no effective date shown) — [Stripe restricted businesses](https://stripe.com/restricted-businesses). VERIFIED CURRENT (that the specific word is absent) / the practical application to peptides is PLAUSIBLE — CONFIRMATION NEEDED.

### Cited Findings — High-risk specialists named as accepting (with conditions)
- **AllayPay** and **QuadraPay** cited as processors surviving peptide vendors reportedly use, with "10% rolling reserves held for 3–6 months, higher fees, and LegitScript certification" — [Project Biohacking, Oct 22, 2025](https://projectbiohacking.beehiiv.com/p/the-fda-s-peptide-crackdown-what-every-biohacker-needs-to-know). PLAUSIBLE — CONFIRMATION NEEDED.
- **Corepay** markets "Peptide Payment Processing Experts (Non-Research Peptides Only)" and states it accepts "medical-grade peptide programs" from licensed healthcare operators, longevity clinics, telehealth — [Corepay](https://corepay.net/industries/best-peptide-merchant-accounts/) (© 2026; "Last audit Jan 2026"; image path suggests 2026/05). VERIFIED CURRENT (that this is their stated position).
- **EasyPayDirect** states you "MUST be LegitScript certified to get a merchant account for Peptides" — [EasyPayDirect](https://www.easypaydirect.com/blog/peptide-merchant-account-fast-and-easy-payment-processing/). PLAUSIBLE — CONFIRMATION NEEDED (vendor).
- **Umbrella Labs** (a SARMs/peptide vendor) press release announcing it began accepting credit cards across its range — but this is datelined **April 25, 2023** (GLOBE NEWSWIRE), names no processor, and is a company self-announcement — [EIN Presswire](https://www.einpresswire.com/article/630048508/umbrella-labs-revolutionizes-the-buying-process-with-the-introduction-of-credit-card-processing-for-sarms-nootropics-peptides-and-research). WEAK-EXCLUDE for "recent" (>12 months old; outside the 90-day/12-month window).

### Inferences
- The processors marketing most aggressively to this niche are brokers/ISOs, not the merchants themselves; "approved" timelines ("live in 5–7 business days," "most merchants live in 5–7 days") are sales claims, not merchant-reported outcomes.
- For an **RUO** positioning specifically, the reachable evidence skews negative: the one specialist with explicit RUO language (Corepay) refuses RUO. Acceptance anecdotes cluster around *clinic/telehealth/medical-grade* framings, not RUO lab-supply framings.

### Gaps
- No reachable first-hand merchant report (Reddit/forum) of a *specific* processor approving or freezing a US RUO peptide seller in the past 90 days.
- No confirmation that AllayPay/QuadraPay currently onboard RUO (vs. medical/clinic) peptide sellers — the citing source is secondary.

## Q3. Recurring compliance requirements merchants/providers mention

### Takeaway
Consistent across independent-ish and vendor sources: **LegitScript certification** (tied to the Visa Integrity Risk Program) is described as effectively mandatory; plus **COAs per product**, **no therapeutic/dosing/efficacy claims**, **institutional-buyer signals**, and correct **MCC coding (5122/5912)**. RUO labeling is explicitly described as NOT sufficient to establish intended use.

### Cited Findings
- **LegitScript certification required:** "As of April 1, 2024, we are unable to assist peptide platforms that are not LegitScript certified," linked to the Visa Integrity Risk Program — [Corepay](https://corepay.net/industries/best-peptide-merchant-accounts/). VERIFIED CURRENT (as stated vendor policy) / the underlying Visa rule is PLAUSIBLE — CONFIRMATION NEEDED (Visa/LegitScript primary not fetched).
- Visa Integrity Risk Program places drug sellers/pharmacies (MCC 5122 and 5912) in its highest tier; LegitScript-certified or NABP-accredited merchants are exempt from parts of the program — [PaylineData](https://paylinedata.com/blog/credit-card-processing-for-peptides/). PLAUSIBLE — CONFIRMATION NEEDED (vendor).
- **COAs:** "A certificate of analysis from a qualified lab is needed for every product," must match the website's product list and be traceable to a real lab; plus supplier agreements and recent invoices — [Binderr](https://binderr.com/marketplace/high-risk-payment-processors-for-peptides). PLAUSIBLE — CONFIRMATION NEEDED.
- **RUO labeling is not a shield:** "a research-use-only label does not settle intended use"; FDA judges objective intent under 21 CFR 201.128 based on claims/advertising/circumstances — [Binderr](https://binderr.com/marketplace/high-risk-payment-processors-for-peptides). PLAUSIBLE — CONFIRMATION NEEDED (but consistent with known FDA doctrine).
- **No claims:** "No dosing or injection instructions, efficacy claims, testimonials, or before-and-after images"; each sale must be legal in both buyer and seller countries — [Binderr](https://binderr.com/marketplace/high-risk-payment-processors-for-peptides). PLAUSIBLE — CONFIRMATION NEEDED.
- **Institutional-buyer signals** help underwriting: institutional emails, purchase orders, invoices to organizations, lab-sized order quantities — [Binderr](https://binderr.com/marketplace/high-risk-payment-processors-for-peptides); echoed by prior search summary. PLAUSIBLE — CONFIRMATION NEEDED.
- **Skincare vs injectable split:** topical cosmetic peptides approved more easily if claims stay cosmetic; injectables are "a different file entirely"; collagen/supplement peptides reviewed as nutraceuticals; GLP-1 sellers hardest — [Binderr](https://binderr.com/marketplace/high-risk-payment-processors-for-peptides). PLAUSIBLE — CONFIRMATION NEEDED.

### Inferences
- The ~$60 AOV / consumer-style RUO model is the *opposite* of the "institutional buyer / PO / lab-sized order" profile underwriters reward — a structural headwind for card approval under current stated requirements.
- LegitScript certification appears to be the single most repeated gating requirement across otherwise-unrelated vendor sources, which raises confidence that it is a real current gate (even if each source is commercial).

### Gaps
- Primary Visa Integrity Risk Program documentation and LegitScript's own current peptide policy were not fetched this session.
- No reachable merchant anecdote confirming what documentation actually got them approved vs. declined.

## Q4. Contradictions (marketing peptide acceptance vs. own AUP; old approvals vs. recent terminations)

### Takeaway
The strongest contradiction is within the high-risk niche itself: processors market "peptide" acceptance while carving out exactly the RUO/research framing this business uses. The mainstream picture (Stripe/PayPal/Square refuse) is internally consistent across sources.

### Cited Findings
- **Corepay markets "Peptide Payment Processing Experts" but excludes RUO:** "We do not support research-use-only (RUO) peptide sellers" and "we cannot support 'for research purposes' peptide platforms" — [Corepay](https://corepay.net/industries/best-peptide-merchant-accounts/). VERIFIED CURRENT (direct quotes from their page). Direct contradiction of the generic "peptides accepted" headline for an RUO merchant.
- **Stripe AUP vs. marketing claims:** Content-farm/vendor pages imply workarounds onto mainstream rails, yet Stripe's restricted-businesses page restricts prescription drugs/online pharmacies and prohibits unsafe/harm-claim nutraceuticals and mislabeled research chemicals — [Stripe](https://stripe.com/restricted-businesses). The vendor "you can still process" framing contradicts Stripe's own stated restrictions.
- **Old approval story vs. current climate:** Umbrella Labs' 2023 "we now accept credit cards" announcement — [EIN Presswire, Apr 25, 2023](https://www.einpresswire.com/article/630048508/umbrella-labs-revolutionizes-the-buying-process-with-the-introduction-of-credit-card-processing-for-sarms-nootropics-peptides-and-research) — predates the 2024–2026 FDA warning-letter wave and the Apr 1, 2024 LegitScript gate; it should not be read as evidence that card acceptance is currently easy or durable.

### Regulatory backdrop corroborating tightening (supporting context, not card data)
- FDA "issued over 50 warning letters in September 2025 alone" (attributed to RAPS); named vendors allegedly warned: Prime Peptides, Summit Research, Swisschems, USApeptide.com — [Project Biohacking, Oct 22, 2025](https://projectbiohacking.beehiiv.com/p/the-fda-s-peptide-crackdown-what-every-biohacker-needs-to-know). PLAUSIBLE — CONFIRMATION NEEDED (secondary; RAPS/FDA.gov primary not fetched).
- Binderr's 2026 timeline: BPC-157/TB-500 to "Category 2" late 2023; FDA action on non-approved GLP-1s (Feb 6); warning to 30 telehealth cos re compounded GLP-1 (Mar 3); 12 peptides removed from Category 2 (Apr 15); advisory committee votes (July); warning letters to five peptide sellers citing bacteriostatic water sold alongside peptides (Aug 24); "as of August 2026 no final rule proposed"; a UK MHRA seizure (May 29, 2026) is not FDA — [Binderr](https://binderr.com/marketplace/high-risk-payment-processors-for-peptides). PLAUSIBLE — CONFIRMATION NEEDED (vendor page; years not always explicit — likely 2026 dates). **Note the "bacteriostatic water sold alongside peptides as evidence of intended human use" point** — directly relevant given this operator keeps bac water live (per project memory).

### Inferences
- The most load-bearing contradiction for decision-making: generic "peptides OK" processor marketing does not survive contact with (a) RUO exclusions and (b) the no-human-use / no-claims / COA-match requirements. An RUO consumer-AOV model is the least-favored sub-segment across every reachable source.
- Selling bacteriostatic water next to RUO peptides is specifically cited by regulators/underwriters as evidence of human-use intent — a flag an acquirer's underwriting would likely weigh against approval.

### Gaps
- Could not confirm any provider that both markets to AND contractually accepts a US consumer-facing RUO peptide seller at ~$60 AOV.
