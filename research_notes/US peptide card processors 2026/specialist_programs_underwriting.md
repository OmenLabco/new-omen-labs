## Which providers have a dedicated peptide / research-chemical merchant account page?

## Underwriting / compliance requirements recurring for RUO peptides

### Takeaway
A consistent checklist recurs across sources: per-product third-party COAs, strict RUO / "not for human consumption" labeling with no therapeutic or dosing claims, full KYC business docs (EIN/LLC, bank statements, IDs, domain proof), processing history, supplier documentation, clear refund/shipping policies, buyer research-use acknowledgment, and — critically for card networks — LegitScript certification and Visa high-risk registration (MCC 5122/5912).

### Cited Findings
- **Per-product COA is near-mandatory.** Underwriters look for independent accredited-lab testing with purity %, batch numbers, visible test date, and a PDF linked on each product page; "a missing COA is one of the most common reasons accounts are flagged." Binderr's checklist requires "a certificate of analysis for every product." — [Fena guide](https://www.fena.co/blog/how-to-sell-research-peptides-on-shopify-uk-compliance-payment-guide-2026-fena); [Binderr](https://binderr.com/marketplace/high-risk-payment-processors-for-peptides)
- **KYC pack:** incorporation documents, owner IDs, 3 months of bank statements, domain proof, supplier agreements, recent invoices, and a review of product pages. — [Binderr](https://binderr.com/marketplace/high-risk-payment-processors-for-peptides)
- **Standard onshore high-risk docs:** government ID, business license, bank statements, processing statements (PaymentCloud); voided check, proof of business + personal bank accounts, prior processing history, fully compliant website, chargeback history (Corepay). — [PaymentCloud](https://paymentcloudinc.com/high-risk-merchant-account/); [Corepay](https://corepay.net/industries/best-peptide-merchant-accounts/)
- **Labeling / claims:** RUO / "not for human consumption" labeling expected; NO therapeutic, dosing, or human-use claims (claims about human use trigger scrutiny and FDA exposure). Payment-provider guidance notes providers "may expect appropriate research-use labeling and clear statements regarding intended use." — [WebSearch: convesio/payfac guidance]; [Payline](https://paylinedata.com/blog/credit-card-processing-for-peptides/)
- **Age verification:** no US statutory age requirement specific to RUO peptides, but high-risk underwriters expect buyer controls — an 18+/21+ site-entry age gate plus a checkout checkbox acknowledging research-only use. — [Fena guide](https://www.fena.co/blog/how-to-sell-research-peptides-on-shopify-uk-compliance-payment-guide-2026-fena)
- **LegitScript certification is the recognized card-network gate.** "Visa and Mastercard recognize LegitScript certification as an approved organization to streamline the high-risk merchant registration process for pharmacies and telemedicine providers that process card-not-present transactions (MCCs 5122 and 5912)." Certification is voluntary/point-in-time; Monitoring is a separate continuous scan processors subscribe to. — [LegitScript healthcare guide](https://www.legitscript.com/healthcare-101-guide); [Convesio LegitScript guide](https://convesio.com/knowledgebase/article/legitscript-certification-guide/)
- **Visa Integrity Risk Program (VIRP):** drug sellers/pharmacies (MCC 5122/5912) sit in Visa's highest tier; acquirers must register these merchants; stated Visa registration fee **USD 950**. — [Binderr](https://binderr.com/marketplace/high-risk-payment-processors-for-peptides); [Convesio](https://convesio.com/knowledgebase/article/legitscript-certification-guide/)
- **Corepay confirms the gate in practice:** since April 1, 2024 it "cannot support 'for research purposes' peptide platforms" that are not LegitScript certified, citing VIRP. — [Corepay](https://corepay.net/industries/best-peptide-merchant-accounts/)
- **Transparency over disguise:** sources repeatedly warn that hiding the real product triggers compliance review and MATCH-listing; present accurately. Payline: "Do not present all peptides as a single approved category or rely on a broad 'gray area' description." — [Payline](https://paylinedata.com/blog/credit-card-processing-for-peptides/); [WebSearch: stonevillenc/syg.ma]
- **Refund/shipping/ToS:** clear refund + shipping policies and terms of service with a buyer research-use acknowledgment appear on multiple application checklists. — [Binderr](https://binderr.com/marketplace/high-risk-payment-processors-for-peptides); [Payline](https://paylinedata.com/blog/credit-card-processing-for-peptides/)

### Inferences
- For omenlabs.co specifically, the single highest-leverage item is almost certainly **LegitScript certification** (or a defensible argument that a pure lab-supply/institutional-research model falls outside MCC 5122/5912). Without it, the identity-verifiable onshore acquirers appear to refuse the category post-April-2024.
- COAs on every product page + a clean RUO/no-human-use site are table stakes just to pass the website review that precedes underwriting.

### Gaps
- Whether a **pure RUO peptide retailer that is NOT a pharmacy or telehealth provider** is formally *required* to hold LegitScript certification (vs. it merely being the practical route acquirers demand) is not settled by the sources; confirm with the acquirer and LegitScript directly.
- FDA warning-letter claims (e.g., "March and August 2026," "FDA removed 12 peptides from Category 2 on April 15 2026") appear only in vendor/commercial pages and could NOT be confirmed against FDA.gov in this research pass — flag as unverified.

## Typical terms for the vertical (reserves, settlement, minimums, fees, setup)

### Takeaway
Published figures are nearly all vendor-reported "typical" ranges, not firm quotes; treat as indicative. Expect high-risk rates in the mid-single digits, rolling reserves of ~5–10% held 120–180 days, per-dispute chargeback fees of ~$20–35, and a one-off Visa high-risk registration fee of USD 950. Most providers mark actual rates/reserves/minimums "not publicly disclosed — set at underwriting."

### Cited Findings (Binderr's stated "typical market figures on the lower side")
- Setup/application fee: **$0–$500** one-off — [Binderr](https://binderr.com/marketplace/high-risk-payment-processors-for-peptides)
- Monthly account fee: **$25–$50** — [Binderr](https://binderr.com/marketplace/high-risk-payment-processors-for-peptides)
- Processing rate: **3.5%–5%** per sale — [Binderr](https://binderr.com/marketplace/high-risk-payment-processors-for-peptides)
- Per-transaction fee: **$0.15–$0.35** — [Binderr](https://binderr.com/marketplace/high-risk-payment-processors-for-peptides)
- Gateway: **$10–$30/mo + $0.05–$0.15/txn** — [Binderr](https://binderr.com/marketplace/high-risk-payment-processors-for-peptides)
- Chargeback fee: **$20–$35** per dispute — [Binderr](https://binderr.com/marketplace/high-risk-payment-processors-for-peptides)
- Rolling reserve: **5%–10% of sales, held 120–180 days** — [Binderr](https://binderr.com/marketplace/high-risk-payment-processors-for-peptides)
- PCI compliance: **$99–$200/year** — [Binderr](https://binderr.com/marketplace/high-risk-payment-processors-for-peptides)
- Visa high-risk registration (one-off): **USD 950** — [Binderr](https://binderr.com/marketplace/high-risk-payment-processors-for-peptides); [Convesio](https://convesio.com/knowledgebase/article/legitscript-certification-guide/)
- Alternative/higher vendor figures: one 2026 commercial article cites **5–8% per transaction, 10% rolling reserve held 6–12 months, $50–$100 chargeback fees** — materially harsher than Binderr; noted as conflicting. — [WebSearch: techbullion/medium 2026]
- PaymentCloud rolling reserve reported by a review site as case-by-case "averaging about 10%." — [WebSearch: fitsmallbusiness/techrepublic]
- Corepay, PaymentCloud, Payline, EPD, Instabill: **rates/reserves/minimums NOT publicly disclosed** — all state these are set at underwriting. — [Corepay](https://corepay.net/industries/best-peptide-merchant-accounts/); [PaymentCloud](https://paymentcloudinc.com/high-risk-merchant-account/); [Payline](https://paylinedata.com/blog/credit-card-processing-for-peptides/)

### Inferences
- At ~$10k/mo volume and $60 AOV (~167 txns/mo), a 5–10% rolling reserve held up to 180 days means roughly $3k–$18k of cumulatively withheld funds at steady state, plus ~$950 Visa registration and possible LegitScript certification cost — a meaningful working-capital drag the merchant should budget for.
- Monthly minimums were not quoted for this vertical specifically; likely folded into the monthly account fee ($25–$50) rather than a separate minimum — but confirm at underwriting.

### Gaps
- No firm, provider-attributable quote (vs. generic range) for peptide monthly minimums, exact reserve % by named provider, or settlement-delay days was found publicly; all "requires direct underwriting confirmation."
- Binderr's ranges and the TechBullion/Medium ranges conflict by roughly 2x on reserves and chargeback fees; neither is an independent benchmark.
