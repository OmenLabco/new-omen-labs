# US High-Risk ISOs, Brokers & Providers for Peptide / Research-Chemical / Nutraceutical CARD Merchant Accounts (2026)

Scope: US merchant (Spokane, WA), US-only customers, research-use-only (RUO) peptides + lab supplies (bacteriostatic water), ~$10k/mo, ~$60 AOV. CARD (Visa/Mastercard) merchant accounts only. ACH/eCheck, crypto, and gateway-only access are noted separately and NOT counted as card acceptance. Access date for all URLs: 2026-10-20.

Evidence labels: VERIFIED CURRENT (provider's own current page states it) / PLAUSIBLE — CONFIRMATION NEEDED / WEAK-EXCLUDE / requires direct underwriting confirmation.

## KQ1: Does each provider explicitly list peptides / research chemicals / nutraceuticals / supplements as SUPPORTED or PROHIBITED? (exact wording + URL)

### Takeaway
The decisive dividing line in 2026 is not "high-risk nutra" marketing but the phrase "research use only" / "for research purposes." Every provider whose own page addresses it either (a) excludes RUO peptide sellers outright (Corepay), or (b) accepts "nutraceuticals/supplements" while listing peptides and SARMs on a banned-ingredients list (PaymentCloud), or (c) accepts peptides only offshore / via unnamed partners (Instabill, Binderr), or (d) markets US peptide acceptance but is a brand-new, unverifiable listing (Midnight Payments). No mainstream US provider's own site was found that affirmatively states it places domestic card accounts for "research-use-only" peptides. LegitScript certification is repeatedly stated as a gating requirement for compliant peptide/pharma models.

### Cited Findings

**Corepay — EXPLICITLY EXCLUDES RUO peptides (VERIFIED CURRENT).**
- "We do not support research-use-only (RUO) peptide sellers." — [Corepay peptide page](https://corepay.net/?p=1130)
- "As of April 1, 2024, we are unable to assist peptide platforms that are not LegitScript certified... This means we cannot support 'for research purposes' peptide platforms." — [Corepay](https://corepay.net/?p=1130)
- What they DO support: "Corepay supports compliant healthcare businesses, longevity clinics, telehealth providers, and medical-grade peptide programs." "We offer peptide merchant accounts and have a strict underwriting process." — [Corepay](https://corepay.net/?p=1130)
- This is a direct, unambiguous exclusion of New Omen Labs' exact business model (RUO peptides). WEAK-EXCLUDE for the RUO model unless the business converts to LegitScript-certified clinical/telehealth.

**PaymentCloud — accepts "nutraceutical/supplement" but peptides & SARMs are on its BANNED-INGREDIENTS list (VERIFIED CURRENT).**
- Banned-ingredients page lists "Peptides" (under P), "Thymosin-β4 and its derivatives e.g. TB-500," plus named peptides Ipamorelin, Sermorelin, CJC-1295, AOD-9604; and SARMs (Ostarine, Andarine, LGD-4033/Ligandrol, RAD-140). Page says these are "either restricted or banned by credit card processors" and "While none of these are illegal, if you sell dietary supplements, you cannot include them in your products." — [PaymentCloud banned ingredients](https://paymentcloudinc.com/blog/banned-ingredients/) (page "Updated: Jan. 30, 2026")
- Nutraceutical industry page: "As a merchant account for nutraceuticals, your products must comply with FDA standards... Banned ingredients or unverified claims are not accepted." — [PaymentCloud nutraceutical-supplements](https://paymentcloudinc.com/industries/nutraceutical-supplements/) (© 2026)
- Net: PaymentCloud accepts compliant supplements but its own list bans the peptides/SARMs that define a peptide store. Does not mention "research chemicals." WEAK-EXCLUDE for peptides; possible fit ONLY for the bacteriostatic-water / non-peptide SKUs. Requires direct underwriting confirmation.

**Instabill — peptides accepted only OFFSHORE, not domestic card (PLAUSIBLE — CONFIRMATION NEEDED).**
- "can now offer peptides merchant accounts through an offshore banking partner." Gift-card payment solution "is ideal for industries such as peptides." Offshore accounts framed for "a high risk industry like research chemicals." — [Instabill peptides](https://instabill.com/ecommerce-industries/peptides-merchant-accounts/) (© 2026)
- No US/domestic card peptide offering described on the page. Alternative offered is a gift-card widget (Apple/Target/Walmart/Amazon) paid out in crypto or ACH — NOT a Visa/MC card merchant account. CAUTION: page footer carries unrelated gambling-spam links ("Hondatoto," "KPKTOTO"), a quality red flag — verify directly.

**Durango Merchant Services — markets nutraceutical/supplement accounts; no peptide/RUO statement found (PLAUSIBLE — CONFIRMATION NEEDED).**
- Own pages present "herbal supplements merchant accounts and nutraceutical merchant accounts" as a served vertical; underwriting checklist covers website requirements (visible address, refund/cancellation policy, ingredient lists, disclaimers), not a product-prohibition list. — [Durango nutraceutical](https://www.durangomerchantservices.com/natural-supplement-nutraceutical-merchant-account/) (page returned 503 on access 2026-10-20; content per search index). Third-party review claims it handles "the full supplement vertical... from basic vitamins to SARMs" — secondary, unverified. No published prohibited-business list located. Requires direct underwriting confirmation on RUO peptides.

**PayKings — markets to nutraceutical/supplement sellers (PLAUSIBLE — CONFIRMATION NEEDED).**
- 2024 press release lists "dietary supplements, vitamins" among industries it serves and claims "24+ acquiring banking relationships." — [PayKings press release (StreetInsider/GlobeNewswire)](https://www.streetinsider.com/Globe+Newswire/PayKings+Introduces+Premier+High-Risk+Merchant+Processing+Solutions+for+Seamless+Approval/23464546.html)
- Competitor Corepay characterizes PayKings as focused on "Nutra, online casinos, and CBD." — [Corepay](https://corepay.net/articles/best-paykings-alternative/) (competitor source; self-interested). No explicit peptide/RUO statement found. Requires direct underwriting confirmation.

**Host Merchant Services — dedicated nutraceutical/supplement page (PLAUSIBLE — CONFIRMATION NEEDED).**
- "Merchant Accounts Built for High-Risk Nutraceutical Brands" — [Host Merchant Services nutraceutical](https://hostmerchantservices.com/nutraceuticals-and-supplement-merchant-account/). Vendor marketing; no approval criteria, no peptide/RUO mention located. Requires direct underwriting confirmation.

**Midnight Payments — explicitly claims US peptide card accounts, but BRAND-NEW / UNVERIFIABLE (WEAK — CONFIRMATION NEEDED).**
- "US-based peptide brands, including startups with no processing history," can get accounts "with no minimum volume and no rolling reserve"; lists "peptides" among industries; "High risk merchant accounts for US businesses," next-business-day settlement, custom pricing. — [Midnight Payments listing (paymentproviders.io)](https://paymentproviders.io/providers/midnight-payments) ("Founded 2026"; profile "Claimed" by provider)
- This is a third-party directory profile, not the provider's own acceptable-use policy; "Founded 2026" and self-reported "90%+ approval" claims are unverified. Does not state LegitScript stance. Treat as a lead to vet, not evidence of a stable program.

**Under.io** — peptide page is now **410 Gone** on access 2026-10-20 ([was](https://www.under.io/blog/peptides-payment-processing)); Under.io is an onboarding/underwriting SOFTWARE platform, not an acquirer — would not itself be the card account.

### Inferences
- New Omen Labs' literal "research-use-only peptides" positioning is the single biggest underwriting blocker. The one provider that addresses RUO head-on (Corepay) refuses it; others that "accept nutra" ban peptides/SARMs by ingredient. Card acceptance for this exact model almost certainly requires either LegitScript certification or re-framing toward a compliant (clinical/telehealth/pharmacy) model — a business decision, not a processor-shopping problem.
- Bacteriostatic water (a lab supply, not a peptide) is far more likely to be acceptable under a standard nutra/supplement account than the peptides themselves. A split-SKU or separate-entity approach may be worth raising with underwriting.

### Gaps
- No provider-own acceptable-use / prohibited-business page was retrievable for EMB, Soar, National Processing, DigiPay, or Leap Payments within the search budget. Their peptide/RUO stance requires direct underwriting confirmation.
- Durango's own nutraceutical page returned HTTP 503 at access time; wording is from the search index, not a live fetch — reconfirm.
- Easy Pay Direct peptide and nutraceutical pages returned 403/HTTP errors on access 2026-10-20; could not verify exact wording directly. Search snippets indicate EPD markets peptide accounts and states "you MUST be Legit Script certified to get a merchant account for Peptides" — needs direct confirmation.

## KQ2: Dated evidence of CURRENT onboarding (prefer last 90 days, else 12 months)?

### Takeaway
Current (2026) onboarding activity is evidenced by page copyright/update stamps and a Sept–Oct 2026 marketplace article, but almost none is provider-dated proof that peptide/RUO merchants specifically are being approved right now. Most date signals are "© 2026" footers, not dated approvals.

### Cited Findings
- PaymentCloud banned-ingredients page carries an explicit "Updated: Jan. 30, 2026" label (source links accessed Aug 2024); strongest dated signal that its peptide/SARM ban is current. — [PaymentCloud](https://paymentcloudinc.com/blog/banned-ingredients/)
- Corepay peptide page shows "© 2026," "Last audit Jan 2026," and the April 1, 2024 LegitScript cutover — indicates the RUO exclusion is a current policy. — [Corepay](https://corepay.net/?p=1130)
- Binderr marketplace peptide article: "Published September 21, 2026; updated October 8, 2026" — within 90 days, but it names only Binderr (broker) as accepting peptides via unnamed partners. — [Binderr peptides marketplace](https://binderr.com/marketplace/high-risk-payment-processors-for-peptides)
- Instabill, PaymentCloud nutra, Host Merchant, Midnight Payments pages show © 2026 but no dated approval evidence. Instabill/PaymentCloud quote onboarding speed ("1-2 business days"; "24–48 hours"; "24 hours to 5 days") — marketing, not dated proof.
- Structural 2025 enforcement tightening (relevant to "is anyone still onboarding RUO peptides"): a Corepay post describes Mastercard's June 2025 bulletin (GLB 11691.1) tightening enforcement against "the sale of unapproved pharmaceuticals, tainted nutraceuticals, and research peptides." — reported via [search index of Corepay]; corroborates that RUO peptides are under active card-network pressure in 2025–2026. Requires direct confirmation of bulletin number.

### Inferences
- The combination of the April 2024 LegitScript/Visa Integrity Risk Program cutover and the June 2025 Mastercard bulletin means the 2026 environment is tightening, not loosening, for RUO peptides. Any "we onboard peptides" claim dated 2026 should be assumed to mean LegitScript-certified / clinical models unless the provider states otherwise in writing.

### Gaps
- No dated, provider-attributed evidence of an actual RUO-peptide approval in the last 90 days was found. "Requires direct underwriting confirmation" applies across the board.

## KQ3: Classification — direct processor/acquirer vs ISO/broker/MAP vs gateway vs payfac (flag referrals)

### Takeaway
Nearly every name on the target list is an ISO / broker / merchant-account provider (reseller that places accounts with sponsor banks/acquirers), NOT a direct acquirer. "Direct processing" language on these sites is marketing; the actual card acceptance sits with unnamed sponsor banks.

### Cited Findings
- Corepay: registered ISO/MSP-style peptide account placer; its page describes its own underwriting but acceptance rides on sponsor acquirers (it ties acceptance to LegitScript + card-network programs). Classify: ISO/MAP. — [Corepay](https://corepay.net/?p=1130)
- PaymentCloud: has an "ISO & Agent" partners section and "Partner Directory"; widely an ISO/reseller (registered ISO/MSP of a sponsor bank), not an acquirer. Page does not self-classify. Classify: ISO/MAP. — [PaymentCloud](https://paymentcloudinc.com/industries/nutraceutical-supplements/)
- Instabill: references an "offshore banking partner," "the acquiring bank we choose," and a partner widget — a broker/referral arrangement, not a direct acquirer. Classify: broker/ISO (offshore-oriented). — [Instabill](https://instabill.com/ecommerce-industries/peptides-merchant-accounts/)
- Binderr: explicitly a broker — "we match your business with a regulated partner that accepts peptides" (partners unnamed). Classify: broker/lead-matcher. — [Binderr](https://binderr.com/marketplace/high-risk-payment-processors-for-peptides)
- PayKings: markets "24+ acquiring banking relationships" — characteristic of an ISO/broker aggregating banks, not a single acquirer. Classify: ISO/broker. — [PayKings PR](https://www.streetinsider.com/Globe+Newswire/PayKings+Introduces+Premier+High-Risk+Merchant+Processing+Solutions+for+Seamless+Approval/23464546.html)
- Midnight Payments: "Our acquiring network approves 90%+..." — an aggregator/broker framing; does not self-classify. Classify: broker/ISO (unverified). — [Midnight](https://paymentproviders.io/providers/midnight-payments)
- Durango, Host Merchant Services, EMB, Soar, National Processing, DigiPay, Leap: all positioned as high-risk merchant-account providers/ISOs (resellers). Durango and EMB in particular are long-standing high-risk ISOs. (General industry knowledge; not re-verified per-page this session — treat classification as PLAUSIBLE.)
- Under.io: underwriting/onboarding SOFTWARE platform, not an acquirer or ISO placing the account.

### Inferences
- Because these are mostly ISOs/brokers, the real question for each is "which sponsor bank underwrites it and does THAT bank accept RUO peptides" — which the ISO sites don't disclose. Expect referrals dressed as direct processing. The merchant must get the acquirer name and the written acceptance of the RUO peptide MCC/product in the merchant agreement before signing.

### Gaps
- Sponsor-bank/acquirer identities behind each ISO are not disclosed on the public pages (standard for the industry). Requires direct underwriting confirmation.

## KQ4: Published fees / reserves / rolling reserve % / settlement delays / contract length / minimums?

### Takeaway
Pricing is essentially never published; every provider uses "custom pricing" set at underwriting. The only concrete numbers surfaced are market-typical ranges from a broker article and a non-target processor's policy, not the target providers' own committed terms.

### Cited Findings
- Corepay: "Peptide merchants pay premium rates"; "lower reserve options" — no figures; "We do not terminate peptide accounts without cause"; calculator outputs are "estimates only." All terms set at underwriting. — [Corepay](https://corepay.net/?p=1130)
- PaymentCloud: "No long-term contracts, no hidden fees, and competitive pricing"; nutra rates "slightly higher due to industry risk"; approvals "24–48 hours." No rate/reserve figures. — [PaymentCloud nutra](https://paymentcloudinc.com/industries/nutraceutical-supplements/)
- Instabill: application is free; no fees/reserves/contract stated; approvals "1-2 business days." — [Instabill](https://instabill.com/ecommerce-industries/peptides-merchant-accounts/)
- Midnight Payments: "no minimum volume and no rolling reserve" (claimed, for US peptide startups); "Custom pricing"; "Next business day" settlement; "the rate, any reserve and settlement timing in writing before you sign." — [Midnight](https://paymentproviders.io/providers/midnight-payments) (unverified directory claim)
- Market-typical ranges (broker article, NOT a target provider's committed terms): "processing from 3.5%," "rolling reserves of 5% to 10%, held 120 to 180 days." — [Binderr](https://binderr.com/marketplace/high-risk-payment-processors-for-peptides)
- Non-target benchmark: Payments.ai requires "at least a 10% rolling reserve for 180 days" on supplement accounts AND bans "CBD/THC, kratom, SARMs, peptides, research chemicals, prohormones." — [Payments.ai nutraceutical restrictions](https://docs.payments.ai/en/articles/13779250-approval-restrictions-for-nutraceuticals-and-supplements). Useful as a reserve/exclusion reference point for what underwriters impose.

### Inferences
- Plan for a high-risk peptide/nutra account to carry premium discount rates (~3.5%+), a rolling reserve in the ~5–10% range held ~120–180 days, and custom/undisclosed contract terms. At ~$10k/mo, a 10% reserve held 180 days ties up roughly $6k in rolling reserve at steady state — material for cash flow. All figures "not publicly disclosed" by the target providers and must be obtained in writing.

### Gaps
- No target provider publishes committed rates, reserve %, settlement delay, contract length, or monthly minimums. All are "not publicly disclosed" / set at underwriting.

## KQ5: US merchant + US-only customers fit?

### Takeaway
A US merchant with US customers is the easy part; it does not rescue the RUO-peptide product problem. The providers that most clearly accept peptides do so OFFSHORE (Instabill) or via unnamed partners (Binderr) — which can mean non-US acquiring, higher cost, and worse fit for US-only card customers. US-domestic card acceptance for RUO peptides was not confirmed at any named provider.

### Cited Findings
- US-domestic peptide claim: Midnight Payments — "High risk merchant accounts for US businesses," US listed as country served, "US-based peptide brands... with no minimum volume and no rolling reserve." — [Midnight](https://paymentproviders.io/providers/midnight-payments) (unverified, brand-new).
- Offshore for peptides: Instabill offers peptides "through an offshore banking partner"; offshore framing for "research chemicals." — [Instabill](https://instabill.com/ecommerce-industries/peptides-merchant-accounts/). Offshore acquiring for US-only retail card customers can raise cross-border decline rates and fees.
- Corepay, PaymentCloud, PayKings, Host Merchant, Durango are US-focused high-risk ISOs — US merchant/US customer fit is fine; the limiter is the RUO-peptide product, not geography.

### Inferences
- Geography is not the constraint. The constraint is product (RUO peptides) + card-network compliance (LegitScript / Visa Integrity Risk Program / Mastercard enforcement). A US merchant should prefer a US-domestic acquirer for US-only card customers (lower declines) — which argues against the offshore peptide routes unless nothing domestic will underwrite the product.

### Gaps
- Whether any US-domestic acquirer will write an RUO-peptide account for a ~$10k/mo Spokane merchant is unconfirmed; requires direct underwriting confirmation.

## Bottom-line for the report-writer
- No named US ISO/broker was VERIFIED CURRENT as placing a US-domestic Visa/MC card account for "research-use-only" peptides. The clearest provider statement on RUO peptides is a refusal (Corepay).
- Best realistic card paths all require a compliance change: LegitScript certification and/or re-framing away from "research use only" toward a compliant model. The bacteriostatic-water / lab-supply SKUs are the most card-acceptable part of the catalog under a standard nutra/supplement account.
- Everything approval-related is "requires direct underwriting confirmation." Candidates worth a direct underwriting call (not endorsements): Durango, PayKings, Host Merchant Services, PaymentCloud (for non-peptide SKUs), and — with skepticism due to being brand-new/unverified — Midnight Payments. Instabill only if an offshore account is acceptable (not ideal for US-only card customers).
