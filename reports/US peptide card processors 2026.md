# No acquirer publicly commits to RUO peptide cards

**Bottom line: after sweeping mainstream payfacs, direct acquirers, high-risk ISOs/brokers, and peptide-named specialist programs, zero US onshore card acquirer was VERIFIED as publicly committing — in writing, in current dated text — to a consumer-facing research-use-only (RUO) peptide store.** The single most on-point provider statement about RUO peptides is a *refusal*: Corepay states verbatim, "We do not support research-use-only (RUO) peptide sellers" ([Corepay](https://corepay.net/industries/best-peptide-merchant-accounts/)). Every mainstream aggregator (Stripe, Shopify Payments, Helcim, PayPal, Square, Adyen) names or catches nutraceuticals / research chemicals / FDA-unapproved products as prohibited or restricted. What remains are a small number of PLAUSIBLE — CONFIRMATION NEEDED specialist leads that must be settled by direct underwriting (chiefly **Payline**, plus **Durango**, **PayKings**, **Host Merchant Services**, **PaymentCloud** for non-peptide SKUs, and the unverified **AllayPay**), and two structural realities that dominate this file: the realistic card paths all require a compliance change (LegitScript certification + Visa high-risk registration under MCC 5122/5912), and two business-specific facts — an RUO consumer framing and bacteriostatic water sold alongside peptides — are precisely the signals underwriters and the FDA read as human-use intent. This report presents those as the researched findings; none of it is advice to disguise or misclassify the business.

## How many credible options exist, and how strong is the evidence

The honest count is **zero fully verified** card paths for the business exactly as it stands (US onshore, consumer-facing, RUO-labeled peptides + bacteriostatic water, ~$10k/mo, ~$60 AOV), and roughly **four to six "worth a direct underwriting call"** leads whose acceptance of *this specific model* is unconfirmed. The evidence base is weak by nature: almost no acquirer publishes a per-vertical risk appetite, and the open web for this niche is saturated with ISO/broker marketing, affiliate SEO, and AI-spun content farms. The strongest, independently-sourced facts in the file are *negative or structural* — the FTC's permanent high-risk ban on the market's biggest nutra acquirer (Humboldt), Visa's Integrity Risk Program tiering, and named mainstream prohibitions — not evidence of anyone saying yes. First-hand merchant testimony (Reddit, Medium) was unreachable this session (403/blocked), so there is no independent approval or termination anecdote for an RUO peptide seller in the last 90 days. Treat every "we onboard peptides" claim as sales framing until a written underwriting approval names the acquirer and the accepted product/MCC.
